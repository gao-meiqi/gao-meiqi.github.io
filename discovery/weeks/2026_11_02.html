01 · Art & Animation

Aichi-Nagoya International Animation Film Festival

Nagoya, Japan · 13–18 November 2026

An international animation festival bringing together animated films, directors, creators and audiences.

Beyond watching films, the festival offers conferences, industry discussions and opportunities to explore the creative process behind animation.

Il Castello dei Mutamenti

Arcidosso, Italy · Artist Residency · 2027

A six-month artistic residency exploring the relationship between art, landscape and local communities.

The programme offers accommodation, workspace and a monthly stipend of €500. The current call is described as a national call for artists and curators under 36. International eligibility should be confirmed.

02 · Music & Stage

Hong Kong Philharmonic — Martin Helmchen

Hong Kong Cultural Centre · 17–18 November 2026

Mozart's Piano Concerto No. 24 in C minor, followed by Bruckner's Symphony No. 4.

A programme worth exploring alongside Schumann's piano music: a different musical language, but equally rich in emotional expression.

Tickets: HK$380–880.

03 · Independent Living

Artist in Residence KOBE

Kobe, Japan · Artist Residency

A residency programme offering artists accommodation, studio access and opportunities to connect with the local community.

This is a self-funded programme rather than a fully funded residency. Accommodation costs ¥120,000 for four weeks. Travel, food and production expenses are not included.

The 2026 open call runs until 30 November.

04 · One Little Discovery

Choreographer-Dancer

A dancer can also be a researcher of movement.

A choreographer observes how the body interacts with space, rhythm, sound and time. Through repetition and experimentation, movements gradually become a language of expression.

I find an interesting parallel here. Scientists study the laws of nature, while choreographers explore the possibilities of the human body.

Perhaps a profession does not always have to be defined by a single identity.

A dancer can be a creator. A scientist can be a photographer, a writer, a musician, or simply someone who keeps exploring other worlds.

There are so many worlds I haven't seen, and so many things I haven't discovered.
