#### I want to learn another way to understand the world.
----
This repository is not about collecting knowledge. It is about understanding how music became a language.

Not only how to hear it,but also how to think with it.

- why a symphony is called a symphony,
- why a sonata is different from a suite,
- why tragedy sounds beautiful,
- why one melody can survive for centuries,
- and how music reflects the way people understand the world.
